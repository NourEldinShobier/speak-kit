## Detailed description

Chrome and Edge, same text (about 1,800 characters; Edge requires at least 250):

```
Speak Kit reads web pages aloud with a natural voice that runs on your own computer. Nothing is uploaded: the text of the page goes only to the Speak Kit desktop app on your machine (127.0.0.1), and the audio is generated there.

Select text and press the shortcut, click the toolbar button to read the whole page, or use the edge launcher on the side of the page. Speak Kit highlights the sentence and the word being read, so you can follow along, and lets you skip, seek, change speed and pick a voice without leaving the page.

What you get
- Read a selection, a paragraph or a whole page.
- Sentence and word highlighting while it reads, with a floating player on the page.
- An edge launcher that stays out of the way until you hover it.
- Reader view: a clean, distraction-free version of the article, in paper, light or dark.
- Queue side panel: line up several pages and jump through the outline of the current one.
- Speed from 0.5x to 3x, several voices, light and dark themes.
- Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+Shift+S read, Ctrl+Shift+Y play or pause, Ctrl+Shift+X stop (Command on Mac; change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts).
- Right-click menu to read a selection or add it to the queue.
- Per-site control: choose where the player appears, or turn it off for a site.

Requires the Speak Kit desktop app
The voice model is too large to ship inside a browser extension, so Speak Kit works together with the free Speak Kit desktop app (Windows), which runs the Kokoro voice model locally and is available from https://github.com/NourEldinShobier/speak-kit/releases/latest. Install the app, start it, copy the pairing token from the app into the extension options, and you are ready. Without the app running, the extension shows how to start it and does nothing else.

Private by design
- No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no remote servers.
- The extension talks only to 127.0.0.1 on your own computer, and only with the pairing token you gave it.
- Settings are stored in your browser; nothing is sent to the developer.

Open source: https://github.com/NourEldinShobier/speak-kit. Report problems at https://github.com/NourEldinShobier/speak-kit/issues.
```

Check before publishing: the features listed are in the current build (speed range, Mac shortcuts, reader themes). If a line is not true of the shipped version, delete it; reviewers compare the listing to the behaviour.
