# SpeakKit logo, round 3: Kit

Built on the scallop-kit tile the owner picked from round 1 (Figma node 1:22): a tile that holds a small kit of soft, wavy pieces. Every piece outline is a sound wave wrapped round a shape, and one warm piece is always the word being read.

Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/laNgdWcGxc1QjZeGnQ74pz, page "Round 3 · Kit" (components under "Round 3 masters").

| Mark | Idea |
|---|---|
| 01 Four voices | The picked tile refined: four pieces, each outline a different voice; blue is speaking, the yellow star is the read word |
| 02 Feature kit | The pieces are what SpeakKit does: voice, highlighted text, page, play; dark tile |
| 03 Puzzle kit | The tile itself is the kit: four pieces cut apart by two voice waves |
| 04 Speech kit | A wavy speech bubble whose dots are three kit pieces; the middle one is yellow |
| 05 Voice deck | Three scalloped voice cards fanned out; the front one is speaking |
| 06 Duo kit | Speak and Kit as two pieces: the voice disc with a warm piece clipped on |

Colours: blue `#2F6BFF`, yellow `#FFC23D`, coral `#FF6B57`, mint `#2FCF9F`, white tile with a soft drop shadow under each piece.

`node kit-marks.mjs [name...]` rewrites `svg/` (uses `../v2/kit.mjs` for path fitting). `round3-board.png` is the Figma board.
